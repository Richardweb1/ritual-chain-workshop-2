Commit-reveal lifecycle Submission privacy flow

  1. Create bounty with reward, rubric, and deadline.

  2. Participants submit only a commitment hash.

  3. After deadline, answers reveal with salt.

  4. Only valid revealed answers enter AI judging.

  5. Owner finalizes winner and reward payout.

Commitment formula Bound to sender and bounty
keccak256(abi.encode(answer, salt, msg.sender, bountyId))

A copied answer cannot be revealed by a different wallet because `msg.sender` is part of the commitment.

Reflection Public, hidden, AI, human

Bounty rules, rewards, deadlines, and final payout should be public so participants can audit the process. Answers and salts should stay hidden during the competitive phase to prevent copying. AI should batch-judge valid revealed answers against the rubric. A human should still confirm subjective or high-context final decisions.
